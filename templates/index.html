Questions people ask

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It estimates risk from a few measurements. Only a blood test ordered by a clinician can diagnose diabetes.

Why is the score not a true probability?

The models were trained with class weights because only 3.76 percent of records are positive. That raises scores for everyone, so read the score as a ranking signal.

Does my district or month change the result?

No. District, year, and month are excluded from the model so that only clinical measurements drive the score.

What happens to fields I leave blank?

They are filled with the median from the training data. The result names each filled field so you know where the estimate is softer.

Can I trust the accuracy numbers?

Treat them with care. The dataset is synthetic and the signal is weak, so the best ROC AUC is well below what a real clinical tool needs. The table above shows every figure.

Is my data saved?

No. Each request is scored in memory and discarded.
